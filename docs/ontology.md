## Edge properties

- `status`: `active`, `planned`, `ceased`, `negated`
- `valid_from`, `valid_to`: the period the relationship held, built from filing dates
- `strength` in (0, 1] with a `strength_basis`, when a filing discloses a percentage
- `confidence`, and the evidence: verbatim `quote`, accession number, filing date, form,
  section and filer

Strength bases:

- `revenue_share`: Share of the supplier's revenue that comes from this customer.
- `purchase_share`: Share of the buyer's purchases of this input that come from this supplier.
- `output_share`: Share of the producer's output committed to this buyer or produced at this facility.
- `ownership_share`: Equity ownership percentage.
- `cost_share`: Share of the buyer's cost of goods sold that this input represents.
- `amount_ratio`: Disclosed amount relative to the recipient's revenue or project cost.
- `sole_source`: Sole, single or limited source with no percentage (fixed strength 0.5).

Fixed priors, used when no strength is disclosed (set before any data, not tuned): strong = 1.0, normal = 0.6, weak = 0.3.

## Relations

Supply-chain relations record which end is **upstream** (the supplier), whatever direction the
relation name reads in. A supply shock flows upstream → downstream; a demand shock flows
downstream → upstream.

23 canonical relations in 6 families. Extraction may also emit `CUSTOMER_OF` (stored as `SUPPLIES` reversed).

### A. Trade links

| Relation | Source → target | Channel | Prior | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SUPPLIES` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = source) | normal | Source sells goods or materials to target. |
| `OFFTAKE` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = source) | strong | Long-term agreement for target to buy source's mine or refinery output. |
| `TOLL_PROCESSES` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = source) | normal | Source converts material that target owns (tolling or contract refining). |
| `SOLE_SOURCES_FROM` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = target) | strong | Source states that target is its sole, single or limited supplier. |
| `LICENSES_TECHNOLOGY_TO` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = source) | weak | Source licenses battery chemistry, cell design or process technology to target. |
| `DISTRIBUTES_FOR` | company → company | supply chain (upstream = target) | weak | Source resells or integrates target's products (for example an energy-storage integrator). |
